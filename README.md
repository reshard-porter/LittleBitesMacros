# LittleBites & Macros

A protein-forward meal planner, smart grocery list and toddler feeding tracker for busy parents.
Built with Swift 6, SwiftUI and SwiftData (CloudKit-ready), targeting iOS 18. No third-party dependencies.

**What's included:** a library of 449 toddler recipes, Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets,
Cooking Mode timers as Live Activities on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and Siri / App Shortcuts.

## Run it

Requires **Xcode 16 or later**.

1. Open `LittleBitesMacros.xcodeproj`.
2. Select the project in the sidebar. For **both** targets (**LittleBitesMacros** and **LittleBitesWidgets**),
   go to Signing & Capabilities and choose your **Team**.
3. Pick an iPhone simulator (iPhone 15 or later shows the Dynamic Island) and press **Run** (⌘R).

To run on your own iPhone, Apple needs unique IDs: change `com.example` in both targets' bundle identifiers, and
change the App Group (`group.com.example.LittleBitesMacros`) in both `Config/*.entitlements` files **and**
`Shared/WidgetSnapshot.swift`.

The project uses Xcode 16 synced folders, so any file added to `LittleBitesMacros/`, `Shared/` or
`LittleBitesWidgets/` is picked up automatically. To rebuild the project file: `python3 Tools/make_xcodeproj.py`.
If you prefer XcodeGen, `xcodegen generate` builds an equivalent project from `project.yml`.

### Optional: multi-caregiver sync
Add **iCloud ▸ CloudKit** and **Background Modes ▸ Remote notifications** to the app target. The store uses
`cloudKitDatabase: .automatic`, so it syncs when the capability is on and uses a local store when it's off.

## Recipe library (449 recipes)

| Slot | Recipes |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | 143 |
| Snacks (morning & afternoon) | 149 |
| Lunch | 118 |
| Dinner | 179 |

Recipes can belong to more than one slot, and they span 21 cuisines. Each recipe has toddler-specific prep steps, a prep time, textures,
and protein, fat, carb and iron estimates. Allergens and choking-hazard guidance come from its ingredients, so they always
match your child's age and allergy profile.

In the app:
- **Fridge & Recipes tab:** search across titles and ingredients. Filter by meal (Breakfast / Snacks / Lunch / Dinner),
  Favorites, Allergy-Safe, Under 15 mins, Leftover Friendly, Finger Food, High Protein, High Iron and cuisine, and sort by
  A–Z, quickest, protein or iron. Hearts sync across caregivers.
- **Add to Plan:** pick the day and meal. For dinners there's a **Cook Once, Serve Twice** toggle that also plans the
  next day's lunch as leftovers.
- **From the plan:** long-press a meal card and choose **Choose a Different Recipe…**, or tap an empty slot.
- **Build a New Week** (⋯ menu): fills all 25 slots from the library, skipping allergens and chaining leftovers on days 2 and 4.
- **Fridge Forager** searches the whole library for quick recipes that use what you picked.

### Editing or adding recipes
The recipes live in `LittleBitesMacros/RecipeLibrary.json`, generated by the Python scripts in `Tools/`:

```bash
python3 Tools/generate_recipes.py
```

- `Tools/recipe_core.py`: the ingredient catalog (116 items, each with nutrition per toddler portion, allergens,
  choking hazard and swap group).
- `Tools/recipes_breakfast_snacks.py` and `Tools/recipes_lunch_dinner.py`: the recipes.
- `Tools/generate_recipes.py`: the default 5-day plan, plus validation (unknown ingredients, duplicate titles,
  invalid enum values, and a minimum of 400 recipes) before it writes the JSON.

Nutrition is calculated by adding up the catalog values for each recipe's ingredients. The 25 recipes in the default
plan keep hand-tuned values.

## Widgets
**Today's Menu** (`LittleBitesWidgets/TodayMenuWidget.swift`):
- **Small / StandBy:** the next meal, with prep time, a leftover badge and a safety badge.
- **Medium:** all five meals for today.
- **Large:** all five meals with prep time, leftover and safe-food notes, and the day's protein and iron.
- **Lock Screen:** rectangular, inline and circular versions.

The timeline moves on to the next meal 45 minutes after each meal's usual time. Tapping the widget opens that day in the app.
The app writes a small `WidgetSnapshot` to the App Group on launch, after plan changes, and when it goes to the background,
so the extension never opens the SwiftData/CloudKit store.

## Live Activities
Start a timer in Cooking Mode. It offers the time it finds in the current step (for example "12–15 min" → 15) plus
2, 5, 10 and 15 minute presets. You then get:
- A Lock Screen banner with the step, a countdown and a progress bar.
- A Dynamic Island view (compact, minimal and expanded) that updates as you move between steps.
- A local notification when the time is up.

The timer keeps running if you leave Cooking Mode, and it's picked up again after the app relaunches (`CookingTimerStore` in `AppServices.swift`).

## Siri & App Shortcuts
| Say | Intent |
|---|---|
| "What's for **lunch** in LittleBites" / "What's my kid's **dinner** in LittleBites" | `WhatsForMealIntent`: reads the meal and a safety prep tip, and shows a card |
| "What's on the menu in LittleBites" | `TodaysMenuIntent`: all five meals |
| "Add **Greek yogurt** to my shopping list in LittleBites" | `AddToShoppingListIntent`: matches the item to the catalog (including lactose-free versions such as "lactose-free Greek yogurt"), or adds whatever you said as a new item |
| "Log a food exposure in LittleBites" | `LogFoodExposureIntent`: asks for the food and the reaction, then logs it to the 15-exposure tracker |

Siri phrases have to include the app name; that's an Apple requirement. All the intents also appear in the Shortcuts app.

## Caveats
- **Not compiled yet.** This code was written in an environment without Xcode. Expect a few small fixes on the first build.
- **Duplicate seed data with CloudKit.** Two devices that both launch before their first sync will each seed the sample data.
  Before shipping, guard seeding with a flag in `NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore`.
- **Rough numbers.** Nutrition values are estimates for a 3-year-old. Safety guidance is general and is not medical advice.
